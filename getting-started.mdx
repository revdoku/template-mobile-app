---
title: Getting started
description: Install the app and sign in.
---

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Open the App Store, search for **Acme** and select **Get**. Requires iOS 17 or later.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Open Google Play, search for **Acme** and select **Install**. Requires Android 13 or later.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Select **Sign in** and enter your work email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    We send a six-digit code to your email. Enter it in the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
