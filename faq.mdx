<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the app work without a connection?">
    Yes. Your changes sync when you are online again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the same account on two phones?">
    Yes. Sign in on each phone with the same email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Open **Settings**, then **Account**, and select **Delete account**. This cannot be undone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
